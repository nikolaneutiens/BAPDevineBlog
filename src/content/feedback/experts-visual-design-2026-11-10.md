  - "Focus on UX and core gameplay before continuing with detailed production."
  - "Define what happens in edge cases, like players losing all their lives or leaving mid-game."

sections:
  - topic: Game Design & UX — Take a Step Back
    feedback: "We should take a few steps back and focus on the core game design and user experience before continuing development. Several important gameplay decisions still need to be explored and defined, and we should not rush into production or take shortcuts before the mechanics and interactions are fully thought through."
    details:
      - "How large should the falling objects be compared to the Boxlings?"
      - "What should the exact design, shape and function of the pressure plates be?"
      - "What happens when one player loses all their lives?"
      - "What happens if one player leaves in the middle of a game?"
      - "What happens if all players leave during a game?"
      - "How should the game respond to each of these situations?"
    next: "Define the gameplay scenarios and edge cases, explore the proportions of the falling objects and finalise the interaction design of the pressure plates."
  - topic: Visual Design — Consistency
    feedback: "The visual style should be more consistent throughout the game. The corrugated edges of cut cardboard are very visible on the trees, but much less on the Boxlings. Because everything belongs to the same cardboard world, these details should feel the same across the environment and the characters."
    next: "Set clear visual guidelines for cardboard materials, textures and cut edges, and compare the environment assets with the Boxlings to find inconsistencies."
  - topic: Backgrounds — Avoid AI-Generated Assets
    feedback: "One of our scenes uses an AI-generated background. Instead, we should build the environment ourselves in Blender, render it and use that render as a 2D background image in Unity."
    next: "Build the environment in Blender, render it from the correct camera angle and use the render as the background in Unity."
  - topic: Logo — Combining 2D and 3D
    feedback: "The AI-generated logo had an interesting visual quality because of its 3D look. Rather than dropping that idea completely, we could combine that 3D look with our own logo design."
    next: "Explore combinations of 2D typography and 3D elements, and develop an original logo that fits the visual identity of the game."
  - topic: Overall Direction — Prioritise UX
    feedback: "There is still a lot of design work to be done. We should take a few steps back instead of immediately adding more assets and features, avoid shortcuts and make deliberate design decisions. For now, user experience and core gameplay are the main priority."
    next: "Re-evaluate the current state of the game, prioritise the open UX and gameplay questions, and document important decisions before implementing them."
