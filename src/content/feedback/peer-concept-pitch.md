sections:
  - topic: Player Count
    feedback: "Why does the game support two or four players, but not three?"
    response: "We explained that supporting three players could be possible and that we would investigate how to implement this."
    next: "Explore three-player gameplay, including the possibility of a computer-controlled fourth character."
  - topic: Physical Controllers
    feedback: "Why not use a half-circle balance board instead of pressure pads?"
    response: "We chose individual pressure pads because we believe they offer a more stable and safer experience for children. A half-circle balance board could increase the risk of losing balance or falling."
    next: "Evaluate controller safety, usability and durability through prototyping and user testing."
  - topic: Connection to Film
    feedback: "How does the concept connect to the JEF Film Festival?"
    response: "The game takes place in a handmade cardboard world created by a fictional stop-motion animator. The characters, props and environments are designed to resemble a stop-motion movie set."
    next: "Make this connection more recognizable through the visual design and storytelling."
  - topic: Starting the Game
    feedback: "How do players join and start a session?"
    response: "We explained how a session starts:"
    responseSteps:
      - "The game waits for players when no one is playing."
      - "A player is ready when standing still with both feet on their designated pressure pads."
      - "Once two players are ready, a countdown begins."
      - "Additional players can join during the countdown."
      - "The game begins automatically when the countdown ends."
    next: "Prototype and test whether this onboarding system is intuitive for children."
  - topic: "Tipper's Motivation"
    feedback: "Why does Tipper need to return to the castle?"
    response: "Tipper collects resources in the surrounding forests and transports them back to the cardboard city."
    next: "Make his motivation clearer through visual storytelling or a short introduction."
